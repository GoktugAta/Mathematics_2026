# Problem 5 Solution

### 1. Computing Ax

```math
Ax =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 \\
1 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
4 \\
2
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
2(4)-1(2) \\
1(4)+3(2)
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10
\end{pmatrix}
```

### 2. Linear Combination of the Columns of A

The entries of x are the coefficients of the columns of A:

```math
Ax =
4\begin{pmatrix}
2 \\
1
\end{pmatrix}
+
2\begin{pmatrix}
-1 \\
3
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
8 \\
4
\end{pmatrix}
+
\begin{pmatrix}
-2 \\
6
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10
\end{pmatrix}
```

Thus, Ax is a linear combination of the columns of A with coefficients 4 and 2.
