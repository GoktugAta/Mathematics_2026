# Problem 5 Solution

### 1. Computing $Ax$

$$
Ax =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 \\
1 & 3 \\
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
4 \\
2 \\
\end{pmatrix} =
\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

### 2. Linear Combination of Columns

The columns of matrix $A$ are:
$$
c_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}, \quad c_2 = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}
$$

Using the entries of vector $x$ as coefficients:

$$
Ax = 4 
\begin{pmatrix}
2 \\
1
\end{pmatrix} 
+ 2 
\begin{pmatrix}
-1 \\
3
\end{pmatrix} 
= 
\begin{pmatrix}
8 \\
4
\end{pmatrix} 
+ 
\begin{pmatrix}
-2 \\
6
\end{pmatrix} 
= 
\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10
\end{pmatrix}
$$
