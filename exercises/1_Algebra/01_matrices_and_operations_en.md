4. What Does Noncommutativity Mean?

Noncommutativity means that changing the order of multiplication can change the result.

In ordinary arithmetic, multiplication is commutative. For example:

2
×
3
=

×
2
=

However, matrix multiplication is generally not commutative:

𝐴

This means that the order of matrices is important when performing matrix multiplication.

Conclusion: Matrix multiplication is generally noncommutative because changing the order of the matrices can produce different results.
